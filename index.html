What does this calculator do?

This tool models a common ESPP strategy:

  • You contribute a percentage of your pay during an offering period.
  • At the end of the period you buy shares at a discount (often with a lookback).
  • You keep a smaller percentage of the shares (as a long-term holding) and sell the rest.
  • The cash from the sale is used to “top up” so your net out-of-pocket is only the Keep % of income.

Profit (after top-up) is the cash result after funding the contribution with sale proceeds.

All tax numbers are simplified estimates only — not tax advice.
